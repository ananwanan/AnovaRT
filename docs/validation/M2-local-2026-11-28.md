The confirmed normal M2 ELF has entry `0x08000141`. Its 304-byte vector table
at `0x08000000` has 512-byte alignment. The verifier matched each vector
to its ELF symbol, checked all reserved words and verified Thumb targets.
Initial MSP is `0x20010000`. `.text` is 11640 B at `0x08000130`,
`.ARM.exidx` is 16 B, `.data` is 4 B at `0x20000000` and `.bss` is 100 B
at `0x20000004`. No allocator symbols were present in the inspected ELFs.
The host frame layout checks confirmed 32 B and LR/PC/xPSR offsets 20/24/28.

Compared with the rebuilt confirmed M1, normal M2 adds 5012 B of Flash,
96 B of static data and a separate 4096 B emergency stack at
`0x20000068..0x20001068`. Normal and legacy images reserve the same 4096 B
initial MSP at `0x2000f000..0x20010000`. The linker and ELF verifier check
alignment and non-overlap. In confirmed normal M2, the direct `m2_fault`
prologue saves 8 B and reserves 464 B before nested formatting calls.
That size is not a peak measurement. All M2 profiles fit the Flash and
static-data targets; stack high-water measurement remains a board activity.

## Hardware and known limits

None of the on-target test firmware paths ran during this record. NVIC
preemption, mask restoration, exception return, actual BusFault routing,
fault logging or silencing, stack high-water mark, 72 MHz and HSI timing,
DS0, UART, reset and cold boot await the
[hardware procedure](M2-hardware-template.md). The bus profile fails with
an explicit panic if the reserved read completes. Faults that cannot
reliably be induced remain unverified as listed in the procedure.
A debugger can perturb timing, and long interrupt masking can lose ticks.
